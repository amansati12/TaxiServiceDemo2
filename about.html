Our Story

Born from a Passion for Better Rides

Founded in 2016, TaxiGo started with just 5 cars and a dream: to make city transportation safer, more comfortable, and completely transparent. Today, we operate a fleet of over 250 vehicles serving 50+ cities across India.

We believe every journey should be a great experience — from the moment you book to the second you step out. That belief drives every decision we make, every hire we bring on, and every upgrade we invest in.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide every passenger with a safe, comfortable, affordable, and dignified travel experience. We exist to serve — with transparency at our core, reliability in our operations, and respect for every individual who steps into our vehicles.

  • Zero compromise on passenger safety
  • Transparent and fair pricing always
  • Create sustainable livelihoods for drivers

Our Vision

To be India's most loved and trusted mobility brand — recognized not just for great rides, but for transforming how people think about urban transportation. We envision a future where every trip, no matter how short, is a moment of care.

  • Expand to 200+ cities by 2028
  • Introduce 100% EV fleet by 2027
  • Become a benchmark in responsible travel
