10 Essential Tips for a Perfect Outstation Road Trip in India

Arjun Patel June 12, 2025 8 min read 4,218 views

Planning your first outstation road trip can feel overwhelming — but with the right preparation, it becomes one of the most memorable experiences of your life. Here are 10 tips that'll make your journey smooth, safe, and spectacular.

1. Book Your Cab Well in Advance

Don't leave vehicle booking for the last minute, especially during festivals, long weekends, or peak travel seasons. Pre-booking not only guarantees availability but also locks in better pricing and lets you choose the exact vehicle type that fits your group and luggage needs.

At TaxiGo, booking 48+ hours in advance also qualifies you for our Early Bird Discount — up to 15% off on outstation packages.

"The best road trips aren't spontaneous — they're spontaneous within a well-planned framework."

2. Choose the Right Vehicle for Your Group

This is one of the most underrated decisions. A sedan works for 2–3 people with light luggage, but for 5–6 people with bags, you'll want an SUV or MPV like the Innova. Cramping into a smaller vehicle for 8+ hours is no way to start a vacation.

Consider these factors when choosing your vehicle:

  • Number of passengers
  • Total luggage (boot space matters on long trips)
  • Terrain — mountain routes need more ground clearance
  • Budget — the extra cost for an SUV is worth the comfort

3. Plan Your Route and Rest Stops

Use Google Maps or similar tools to identify your route, but more importantly, mark rest stops every 2–2.5 hours. Look for dhabas, petrol stations with clean restrooms, or highway restaurants. Share the planned route with a family member who isn't travelling.

4. Check the Weather Forecast

Indian weather can be unpredictable. Check the forecast for all cities on your route 2–3 days before departure. If you're heading to hilly areas, be especially cautious during monsoon months (June–September) — landslides and road closures are common.

5. Carry Essential Documents

Your driver will carry the vehicle's RC, insurance and PUC documents, but as a passenger, ensure you carry a valid government-issued ID (Aadhar, Passport, or DL). Some states and tourist spots require passenger ID checks.

6. Communicate Your Itinerary to Your Driver

The night before your trip, share the full itinerary with your driver — all stops, detours, and expected timings. A well-briefed driver can plan fuel stops, meals, and rest breaks more efficiently. TaxiGo drivers are all experienced in outstation travel and can often suggest better routes or interesting pit stops.

7. Pack a Road Trip Emergency Kit

Pack a small bag with: a first-aid kit, basic medicines (ORS, painkiller, antacid), extra phone charger and power bank, cash (in case of no signal), water bottles, and a change of clothes. These small preparations can save the day on long drives.

8. Keep Snacks and Water Handy

Don't rely entirely on highway dhabas. Pack dry snacks, fruits, and water for everyone in the car. This is especially important if you're travelling with children or elderly passengers. Dehydration is a common problem during long drives in Indian summers.

9. Take Breaks — Don't Rush

The biggest mistake most road trippers make is treating it as a race to the destination. Build buffer time into your itinerary. Stop at that random lake you spot. Get out and stretch. Explore the local chai stall. The unplanned moments are often the ones you'll remember most.

10. Trust Your Driver's Advice on Road Conditions

Experienced outstation drivers know the roads like the back of their hand. If your TaxiGo driver suggests an alternate route due to traffic, road conditions, or weather — listen to them. Their local knowledge is an invaluable asset that no navigation app can fully replicate.

"A great road trip is 30% destination and 70% everything that happens on the way."
